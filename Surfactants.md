*a.k.a ligands, capping agents*
Molecules that encapsulate nanoparticles and prevent them from aggregating. As an example, consider the CdS and CdSe nanoparticles that have been synthesized. As I understand it, two capping agents have been used, namely [oleylamine](#Oleylamine) and [oleic acid](#oleic%20acid). They are typically used to suspend the nanoparticles in [octadecene](#octadecene) and/or [toluene](#toluene) solvents. 
## Oleylamine
#ligand
- Exhibits affinity to metals through NH2 functional groups. 
- Acts as strong reducing agent as well as a stabilizer in the synthesis of nanoparticle.

## Oleic Acid
#ligand
- Widely used as a surfactant for the stabilization of magnetic nanoparticles.
- Carboxyl group binds to metallic NPs in one of a number of different configurations.
![[oleic_acid_structure.png]]
## BMEP
#ligand 
- Hydrophilic monomer 
- Two polymerizable methacrylate groups 
- Centrally placed phosphate group that binds to NPs.
- It is widely used as a precursor in the synthesis of poly (BMEP), dental resins, hydrogels, and fire-retardant polymer composites.

### Chemicals 
- Oleic acid (OA, 90%)
- 1-octadecene (ODE, 90%), 
- ytterbium oxide (99.9%), 
- `bis[2-(methacryloyloxy)ethyl] phosphate (BMEP)`,
- vinyl toluene (VT, 99%,), 
- divinylbenzene (DVB, 80%), 
- 2-(4-tertbutylphenyl)-5-(4-biphenylyl)-1,3,4-oxadiazole (b-PBD, 99%) 
- 1,4-bis(5-phenyloxazol-2-yl) benzene (POPOP) 
- trifluoroacetic acid (TFA)
- 1,1-bis(tertbutylperoxy)-3,3,5-trimethylcyclohexane (DTTMC, 75% in aromatic free mineral spirit)
- EJ-212
### Precursor Yb(CF3COO)3 synthesis 
- Yb2O3 (10 g) powder, trifluoroacetic acid (23 ml) and DI water (23 ml) added to 250 ml flask. 
- Heated to 65°C stirred for 10 h.
- Excess trifluoroacetic acid removed by rotation evaporation to get white powder.
- Powder then dried by purging with dried argon under heating for 2 h.  
### YbF3 NP synthesis  
- Oleic acid (33.9 g) and 1-octadecene (30.3 g) added to 250 ml three-neck flask
- Degassed under vacuum for several minutes with periodic argon purging. 
- Yb(CF3COO)3 (3 g) added into the mixture under argon. 
- Solution heated to 100°C in 30 min under argon
- Vacuum pumping applied for several minutes to remove residual water and oxygen. 
- Solution was rapidly heated to 304°C and maintained at this temperature for 45 min. 
- Mixture was cooled to room temperature
- ==NPs were precipitated by the addition of acetone/hexane (4 : 1 v/v) and isolated via centrifugation at a speed of 7000 rpm for 5 min.== 
- Resulting white powder washed with ethanol three times and dried under vacuum. 
- Resulting NPs could dissolve in a low-polarity solvent well.  
### Ligand exchange of oleic acid-stabilized YbF3 NPs with BMEP  
- NP white powder (1 g) dissolved in chloroform (toluene) (10 ml)
- BMEP (59 mg) was added. 
- Kept in ice bath and for 3 h. 
- Solvent removed by rotation evaporation
- Ethanol added to precipitate the NPs, followed by centrifugation. 
- BMEP-exchanged NPs dried under vacuum at room temperature.  
### Polymer nanocomposite fabrication  
- 2 ml glass vial with 10 mm diameter modified to get a hydrophobic inner surface. 
	- Filled with hydrogen peroxide for 1 h
	- Rinsed by DI water and dried. 
	- Treated with 3 wt% solution of dimethyldichlorosilane in chloroform 30 min and dried. 
- VT monomer and DVB purified using inhibitor remover.
- For the polymer nanocomposite fabrication, 
	- Ligand exchanged NPs, 
	- a solution of VT containing 5 wt% of DVB and 
	- 1 wt% of thermal initiator DTTMC added to a Schlenk flask. 
- For the scintillator fabrication, 
	- 2 wt% of PBD and 0.02 wt% of POPOP additionally added. 
- Mixture sonicated for 15 min in ice bath and became transparent. 
- Air in Schlenk flask removed by vacuum and filled with argon. 
- Solution transferred to treated glass vial in glove box
- Capped and cured at 90°C for 24 h. 
- Transparent NP/PVT composite monolith demolded and polished.
